Key Findings

↓ 50% Token Reduction

SRAM v1.0 30B consumes 25.8–95.3% fewer reasoning tokens compared to 30–32B agentic LLMs achieving similar accuracy.

△ Better RL Initialization

Self-regulation SFT reduces RL token consumption by 41–55% while converging to a higher overall score than black-box training.

∞ Deeper, Not More, Planning

RL increases average plan length by 22.8% while raising planning frequency only 2.0% — the model learns to plan better, not more.

↗ Scales Above the Trend

Both SRAM models sit above the Overall Pass@1 vs. log(parameter size) trendline, outperforming their scale would predict.
